# Provisional Pattern Library v0.1

## Shared grammar
ATTENTION → PRODUCT → HUMAN/CONTEXT → PROOF/DEMONSTRATION → BENEFIT/CLAIM → ACTION → BRAND

## Families
### A — Polished Product
Product-led visual presentation with controlled composition and product detail.

### B — Presenter UGC
Human/presenter-led product communication with product interaction.

### C — Expert/Treatment
Expert or treatment-context presentation where demonstration and explanation carry the story.

### D — Macro Demo
Close/macro product action, dispensing/application and visible proof.

### E — Brand/Launch
Brand-led announcement, campaign or launch communication.

All patterns are provisional until validated by controlled experiments.
